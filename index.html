Gemini Nano isn't enabled — here's how to fix it

OffQuill needs Gemini Nano to paraphrase text. It's built into Chrome but needs two flags turned on.

1
Open Chrome flags
chrome://flags/#optimization-guide-on-device-model
Paste this into your Chrome address bar and press Enter
2
Set it to Enabled BypassPerfRequirement Find the dropdown and select "Enabled BypassPerfRequirement" (this skips hardware checks)
3
Open the second flag
chrome://flags/#prompt-api-for-gemini-nano
Paste this into your Chrome address bar and press Enter
4
Set it to Enabled Same as before — find the dropdown, select "Enabled"
5
Relaunch Chrome Click the "Relaunch" button that appears at the bottom, or just quit and reopen Chrome

After relaunching, come back here and refresh the page. The banner will disappear.
